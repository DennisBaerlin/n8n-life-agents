# What can I do for you?

Just write in plain language what you need – here are the areas with examples:

## Accounts
Bank accounts, portfolios, fixed deposits: open, change, look up the current interest rate on the web, reconcile the balance, close.
- "Close the Trade Republic Cash current account, closing date today."
- "Open a new Comdirect current account, I am the holder, no interest."
- "Reconcile my DKB Giro: according to the banking app the balance today is exactly 1,234.56 euros."

## Bookings & transfers
Book expenses/income, transfer between your own accounts, reverse wrong bookings.
- "I paid 85 euros for groceries with the DKB Giro today. Please book it as an expense."
- "Transfer 500 euros from the DKB Giro to the Trade Republic Cash current account, date today."
- "Reverse my last booking on the DKB Giro."

## Portfolio & securities
Stock/ETF/crypto purchases and sales, dividends, savings plans.
- "Book a dividend of 12.50 euros for my Vanguard FTSE All-World position (VWCE) in the Trade Republic portfolio to the Trade Republic Cash current account, date today."
- "The broker executed my VWCE savings plan today: 0.44 shares at 113.50 euros per share, order fee 0 euros."
- "Buy 10 shares of Vanguard FTSE All-World (ISIN IE00BK5BQT80, symbol VWCE) in the Trade Republic portfolio at 110 euros per share, order fee 1 euro, purchase date today."

## Loans
Record loans, book instalments, change terms, repay early.
- "Book the monthly instalment for my mortgage, instalment 1,200 euros, date today."
- "My ING mortgage has a new interest rate of 4.1% after the fixed-rate period ended. Please update it."

## Insurance
Record policies with their premium, change them, cancel them.
- "Cancel my HUK household contents insurance."
- "Record a new liability insurance with HUK-COBURG, policy number HUK-9876, annual premium 89 euros, the holder is my wife, debited from the DKB Giro, valid from 1 Jan 2026, paid yearly on the 15th, first debit on 15 Jan 2026."
- "The annual premium of my HUK household contents insurance goes up to 95 euros. Please update it."

## Retirement provision
Record state/occupational/private pensions as assets.
- "Record my Rürup pension with Allianz, current surrender value 12,000 euros (as of today), monthly contribution 200 euros from the DKB Giro, debit day 1."

## Real estate
Add property as an asset, revalue it, sell it.
- "Please delete the property 'Musterimmobilie' completely from my net worth overview, I don't need it any more."
- "Update the value of my Musterimmobilie to 350,000 euros."

## Savings goals
Savings goals with a target amount/date and an automatically calculated savings rate.
- "Create a new savings goal 'Emergency fund': target 10,000 euros by the end of 2027, from the DKB Giro into Tagesgeld Plus, debit day 1, first booking on 1 Jul 2026."

## Standing orders
Manage recurring payments (salary, rent, subscriptions, pension).
- "Add a recurring expense: Netflix subscription, 13.99 euros monthly from the DKB Giro, debit day 5, first payment on 5 Jul 2026."
- "Set up a monthly standing order: 200 euros from the DKB Giro to the Trade Republic Cash current account, from 1 Jul 2026, on the 1st of the month."

## Import
Import bank statements/policies/pension statements as a photo or as bulk data.

## Advice & planning
The financial adviser gives recommendations, answers “is it worth it?/should I?”, researches with dated sources on the web and maintains your financial plan (interview + quarterly review).

## Questions & reports
Ask directly for figures: account balance, net worth overview, monthly cash flow, remaining debt (“what is my balance on the DKB Giro?”).

## Job search & matching
Find and rate suitable jobs, bookmark interesting matches.
- "Please bookmark this job: Scrum Master at Beispiel GmbH in Hamburg."

## Career advice & planning
The career coach advises on changing jobs, maintains your career profile (current role, skills, experience) and your career plan (target roles, target industries, timeline for the change) and researches with dated sources on the web.

## Estate & provision
Record and maintain provision documents, contracts, digital accounts, key contacts and locations in the emergency register.
- "I have just gone through our emergency register for Erika, everything is still up to date — please mark it as reviewed."
- "Please save our notary as a key contact: Notariat Dr. Muster in Musterstadt, phone 0123 456789."
- "Add my GMX email account to the digital estate, it is my key account, the access is in the Bitwarden password manager with the master password in the safe."

## Estate & provision (advice)
The estate adviser gives the household an overview of its provisions (provision documents, contracts with customer numbers, digital estate without passwords, key contacts, funeral wishes, where things are kept) and records and maintains them in the emergency register. In an emergency case (death/incapacity) it guides you with care: a step-by-step checklist with legal deadlines that the database calculates to the day (registry office, insurance notification deadlines, disclaimer of inheritance, inheritance tax, ...), 'one next task after another', a daily deadline reminder and a look into the register. The printable EMERGENCY BINDER (the whole register as one forwardable document) and the system profile (where the offline copy of the backup key is kept) give the bereaved everything they need — even when the system is off; the command /notfall is the fixed entry point that does not depend on the AI. It does not replace legal advice.

## Documents
Find and read documents in your Paperless instance and get questions about them answered (read-only, adults only).
- "Ask the document AI what notice period my mobile phone contract has."
- "Read me the document with number 42."
- "Find my rental contract in the documents."

## Reminders & monitoring
Triggers on any topic: thresholds, drawdowns, dates, regular checks.
- "Please keep an eye on the gold price for me."
- "Which monitoring triggers are active right now?"
